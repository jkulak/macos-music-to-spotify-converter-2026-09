How it works
============

Music.app is read once, every track is looked up on Spotify, and the
playlists are written to your account. Every step is safe to run again:
searches are cached, decisions live in data/overrides.csv, and a sync only
touches the playlists it created.

  1. make export            Snapshot the Music library into data/library.json.
  2. make match-until-done  Find every track on Spotify (log in once first
                            with make auth); sleeps out the daily quota.
  3. make ui                Decide what matching could not: p / m play both
                            versions, y takes, n not on Spotify, x leaves out,
                            t searches YouTube, ? lists the keys. Or take all
                            proposals unseen with make accept-all.
  4. make apply             Push the decisions, create or update the
                            playlists (make plan previews them, no writes).
  5. make sync PLAYLIST_NAMES=plain
                            Once the playlists sit in their Spotify folders,
                            drop the folder path from their names.

Also
  make youtube-match        Find the favourites Spotify lacks on YouTube Music;
                            youtube-auth once, youtube-sync fills a playlist.
  make archive-matched      Dry run of moving the mp3s now on Spotify out of
                            Music; CONFIRM=yes does it.
  make help                 Every target, make summary among them.

reports/ lists what is missing from Spotify (report.md, not-found.csv);
data/ holds the snapshot, the matches and your decisions, so back it up.
